Comprehensive Syllabus Breakdown

CA, CMA, CS & ACCA Course Structure

Official level-by-level paper structure for Chartered Accountancy, Cost Accounting, Company Secretary, and ACCA qualifications.

CA Classes (Chartered Accountant)

Offline Classes / Online Classes available for CA Foundation, CA Intermediate, and CA Final levels.

LEVEL 1: CA FOUNDATION

  • PAPER 1: ACCOUNTING
  • PAPER 2: BUSINESS LAWS
  • PAPER 3: QUANTITATIVE APTITUDE
  • PAPER 4: BUSINESS ECONOMICS

LEVEL 2: CA INTERMEDIATE

GROUP 1

  • PAPER 1: ADVANCED ACCOUNTING
  • PAPER 2: CORPORATE AND OTHER LAWS
  • PAPER 3: TAXATION

GROUP 2

  • PAPER 4: COST AND MANAGEMENT ACCOUNTING
  • PAPER 5: AUDITING AND ETHICS
  • PAPER 6: FINANCIAL MANAGEMENT AND STRATEGIC MANAGEMENT

LEVEL 3: CA FINAL

GROUP 1

  • PAPER 1: FINANCIAL REPORTING
  • PAPER 2: ADVANCED FINANCIAL MANAGEMENT
  • PAPER 3: ADVANCED AUDITING, ASSURANCE AND PROFESSIONAL ETHICS

GROUP 2

  • PAPER 4: DIRECT TAX LAWS AND INTERNATIONAL TAXATION
  • PAPER 5: INDIRECT TAX LAWS
  • PAPER 6: INTEGRATED BUSINESS SOLUTIONS

CMA Classes (Cost & Management Accountant)

LEVEL 1: CMA FOUNDATION

  • PAPER 1: FUNDAMENTALS OF BUSINESS ECONOMICS AND MANAGEMENT
  • PAPER 2: FUNDAMENTALS OF ACCOUNTING
  • PAPER 3: FUNDAMENTALS OF LAWS AND ETHICS
  • PAPER 4: FUNDAMENTALS OF BUSINESS MATHEMATICS AND STATISTICS

LEVEL 2: CMA INTERMEDIATE

  • PAPER 1: BUSINESS LAWS AND ETHICS
  • PAPER 2: FINANCIAL ACCOUNTING
  • PAPER 3: DIRECT AND INDIRECT TAXATION
  • PAPER 4: COST ACCOUNTING
  • PAPER 5: OPERATIONS MANAGEMENT & STRATEGIC MANAGEMENT
  • PAPER 6: COST & MANAGEMENT ACCOUNTING AND FINANCIAL MANAGEMENT
  • PAPER 7: CORPORATE ACCOUNTING AND AUDITING
  • PAPER 8: DATA ANALYTICS IN BUSINESS

Company Secretary (CS) Classes

LEVEL 1: CS EET (Executive Entrance Test)

  • PART 1: BUSINESS COMMUNICATION
  • PART 2: LEGAL APTITUDE AND LOGICAL REASONING
  • PART 3: ECONOMIC AND BUSINESS ENVIRONMENT
  • PART 4: CURRENT AFFAIRS AND QUANTITATIVE APTITUDE

ACCA (Association of Chartered Certified Accountants)

APPLIED KNOWLEDGE & SKILLS LEVELS

  • Paper 1: BT – Business & Technology
  • Paper 2: MA – Management Accounting
  • Paper 3: FA – Financial Accounting
  • Paper 4: LW – Corporate & Business Law
  • Paper 5: PM – Performance Management
  • Paper 6: TX – Taxation
  • Paper 7: FR – Financial Reporting
  • Paper 8: AA – Audit & Assurance
  • Paper 9: FM – Financial Management
